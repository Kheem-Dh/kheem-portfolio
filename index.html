I build and evaluate AI systems for healthcare. At Clinical Pearl, a US healthcare product company, I lead a team of three engineers building a production retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) platform that answers clinical questions for more than 1,000 clinicians. My work there spans retrieval and grounding, LLM evaluation and observability, and evidence-backed biomedical knowledge sources built from FDA drug data, RxNorm and SNOMED CT.

My research interest is trustworthy language models for clinical use: methods to evaluate, ground and calibrate LLMs and RAG systems so their outputs can be checked against evidence. I developed two evaluation frameworks for clinical AI, CAMEO-AI and PEARL-G, presented at EBM Live, Oxford (2026). Before that, my MS thesis at FAST-NUCES combined vision transformers and language models for radiology report generation on MIMIC-CXR. I have three peer-reviewed publications in applied NLP and multimodal learning.

I am looking for a fully funded PhD position (Fall 2027) in trustworthy and clinical AI. Please get in touch if my work fits your group.
